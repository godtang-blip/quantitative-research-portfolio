# Research Limitations

- Daily OHLC cannot establish the actual intraday sequence or guarantee a fill when a limit is touched. Queue position, market impact, partial fills, liquidity and venue-specific execution costs remain unresolved.
- The equity universe used later survivors. Both historical windows were previously examined. Independent holdout testing and corrected full-period validation are outstanding.
- The earlier v5.3 return comparison failed. The exact historical execution revision, inputs and daily NAV remain incomplete.
- The BTC study varies one parameter axis at a time. Its research design leaves parameter interactions and multiple testing unresolved. The paper-trading evidence covers only archived output with no fills.
